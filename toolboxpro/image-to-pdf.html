Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! You can upload multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP pictures at once, arrange them in any order using the Up and Down buttons, and export them into one clean PDF.

ToolBoxPro supports standard A4, US Letter, and 'Fit to Image' (which sizes each PDF page to match the exact dimensions of that photo).

No software or plugins are required. Everything runs directly inside your web browser on mobile phones, tablets, or computers.

No. ToolBoxPro outputs 100% clean, watermark-free PDFs suitable for university submissions, job applications, and official paperwork.

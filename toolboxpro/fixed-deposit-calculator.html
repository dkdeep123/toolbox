Frequently Asked Questions

How is Fixed Deposit (FD) interest calculated in Indian banks?

Indian commercial banks calculate Fixed Deposit interest using quarterly compounding for tenures of 6 months and above. The formula is A = P × (1 + r / 400)^(4 × t), where P is principal, r is annual interest rate, and t is tenure in years.

Do Senior Citizens get higher FD interest rates?

Yes, most Indian banks offer an additional 0.50% (50 basis points) higher interest rate to senior citizens (individuals aged 60 and above) across domestic term deposits.

What is the difference between Cumulative and Non-Cumulative FD?

In a Cumulative FD, interest is compounded quarterly and paid out along with the principal at maturity, maximizing wealth growth. In a Non-Cumulative FD, interest is paid out periodically (monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly) into your savings account.

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes, interest earned on fixed deposits is taxable as per your applicable income tax slab. Banks deduct TDS at 10% if total interest exceeds ₹40,000 per year (₹50,000 for senior citizens) unless Form 15G or 15H is submitted.

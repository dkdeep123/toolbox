Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF to an exact custom size in KB?
Enter your target limit (e.g. 100, 200, 300, 500 KB) in the custom input box or click any portal preset chip. Our intelligent multi-page adaptive engine automatically determines optimal rendering scale and JPEG quality to guarantee your output PDF is strictly under your target.
Which government exams and portals require PDFs under 200KB or 300KB?
NTA NEET UG/PG, JEE Main, and CUET typically mandate caste, category, and PwD certificates between 50KB and 300KB. SSC and UPSC OTR document uploads require under 200KB or 300KB. RRB Railway pass and GATE applications typically cap certificates at 500KB.
Is it safe to compress confidential legal, identity, or marksheet PDFs on ToolBoxPro?
Yes, 100%. ToolBoxPro operates entirely client-side using JavaScript. Your files are processed in local memory and are never transmitted over the internet or saved to cloud servers.
Will compressing my PDF blur the text or signatures?
Vector text and fonts remain crisp. For scanned certificates, our adaptive engine preserves optimal DPI and high-contrast rendering so stamps, numbers, and signatures stay clearly legible for official portal verification.
What is the maximum file size supported?
Because processing runs in browser memory, files up to 50MB compress smoothly on standard desktop and mobile browsers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculation include both the start and end dates?
Standard duration measures the elapsed difference between dates. You can count the final day as inclusive or exclusive depending on legal contract terms.
How does the calculator handle leap years?
All calculations use the Gregorian calendar standards, automatically incorporating February 29th whenever the date range spans a leap year.
Can I calculate past date intervals?
Yes. You can measure historical spans between any two historical dates.

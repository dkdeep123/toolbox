Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between RGB, HEX, and HSL?

RGB defines colors by red, green, and blue light intensity (0–255). HEX is the hexadecimal shorthand representation of RGB used in web design (e.g. #5B4FFF). HSL represents Hue (0–360°), Saturation (0–100%), and Lightness (0–100%), which is more intuitive for human color adjustment.

What is the WCAG accessibility contrast ratio requirement?

WCAG 2.1 Level AA requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Level AAA requires a stricter 7:1 ratio for normal text.

What is CMYK used for?

CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/Black) is a subtractive color model used in commercial physical printing, contrasting with additive RGB light displays.

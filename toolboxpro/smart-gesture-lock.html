Frequently Asked Questions

Why is PWA app installation required?

Smart Gesture operates as a continuous device sensor utility. Installing ToolBoxPro as a Progressive Web App (PWA) gives it standalone execution, background lifecycle management, and full-screen display control without browser address bar clutter.

Is my camera video recorded or uploaded?

No. Never. Video frames are streamed strictly into an offscreen HTML5 canvas inside your device's RAM, evaluated frame-by-frame with optical algorithms, and discarded immediately. No data ever leaves your device.

How does it lock my phone?

When the gesture confidence reaches 100%, ToolBoxPro activates a pitch-black OLED fullscreen overlay, plays authentic mechanical lock clicks via the Web Audio API, pulses haptic vibration, and releases Screen WakeLock so the phone sleeps naturally.

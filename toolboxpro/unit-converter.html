Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
The formula is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9.
How many pounds are in one kilogram?
1 kilogram is approximately equal to 2.20462 pounds (lbs).

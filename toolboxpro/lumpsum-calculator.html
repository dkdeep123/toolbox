Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Lump Sum Investment?

A Lump Sum investment is a single, one-time bulk capital deposit into a mutual fund scheme or financial instrument, as opposed to periodic contributions like a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP).

What formula is used to calculate Lump Sum returns?

The mathematical formula for Lump Sum maturity is: A = P × (1 + r / 100)^t, where A is the maturity value, P is initial principal invested, r is expected annual rate of return, and t is tenure in years.

When should you invest Lump Sum instead of SIP?

Lump Sum investing is ideal when you receive a large windfall or bonus, have surplus cash, or when stock markets have experienced a significant correction, allowing you to buy mutual fund units at lower NAVs.

What return rate should I expect from mutual fund lump sum?

Over long periods (7–10+ years), diversified Indian equity mutual funds (Nifty 50 Index, Flexi-cap, Large & Mid Cap) have historically delivered 11% to 14% annualized returns, while debt funds typically deliver 6% to 8%.

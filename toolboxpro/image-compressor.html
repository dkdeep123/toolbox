Frequently Asked Questions

Does compressing an image lower its resolution or pixel dimensions?
No. By default, ToolBoxPro preserves the exact width and height of your original image, stripping redundant metadata and optimizing color quantization to reduce file size.
Which format yields the best compression?
Modern WebP typically achieves 25% to 35% smaller file sizes than standard JPEG at equivalent visual quality. You can choose WebP in the format dropdown for maximum savings.
Are my uploaded photos stored on your servers?
Never. Compression runs entirely within your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your pictures never leave your local phone or computer.
Why did my PNG file not compress as much as a JPEG?
PNG is a lossless compression format suited for graphics and text with transparent backgrounds. If your picture is a detailed photograph, converting it to JPEG or WebP will yield dramatic 70%+ file reductions.

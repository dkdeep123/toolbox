Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard tipping percentage at restaurants?
15% is standard for adequate service, 18% for great service, and 20% or more for exceptional dining experiences.
Should tips be calculated on the pre-tax or post-tax bill?
Etiquette guides generally recommend calculating gratuity on the pre-tax subtotal, though tipping on total with tax is also widely accepted.
Can this split uneven bills?
This tool computes mathematically equal splits across the party. For itemized splits, diners can compute their portion plus tip percentage.

1. Financial Tools Disclaimer (EMI, SIP, GST, Salary)

ToolBoxPro provides interactive financial calculators to assist users in understanding loan schedules, compounding growth, taxation percentages, and net salary breakdowns:

  • Loan EMI & Amortization: Formulas follow standard reducing-balance mathematical equations. However, actual loan interest, processing fees, loan rest periods (daily, monthly, or annual reducing), and prepayment penalties vary across specific commercial banks and financial lending institutions.
  • SIP & Mutual Funds: Systematic Investment Plan projections use theoretical compounded annual growth rates (CAGR). Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, volatility, expense ratios, and capital gains taxation. Historical projections do not guarantee future performance.
  • GST Calculations: GST computations simulate current standard Indian Goods and Services Tax slabs (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%). Businesses must confirm exact classification codes (HSN/SAC) and applicable cess with a certified chartered accountant or the official GST portal (gst.gov.in).
  • Salary Calculator: Deductions for Provident Fund (EPF), Professional Tax, and Income Tax regimes are simulations. Users should consult their employer payroll or tax advisor for definitive take-home pay analysis.

2. Health & Fitness Tools Disclaimer (BMI Calculator)

The Body Mass Index (BMI) calculator provides an estimation of body mass categorization according to World Health Organization (WHO) reference thresholds:

  • BMI is a statistical screening measure and does not directly measure body fat percentage, bone density, or muscle mass distribution.
  • Athletes, pregnant or lactating women, elderly individuals, and growing children should not use standard adult BMI as a definitive diagnosis of health.
  • Always consult a licensed physician, clinical nutritionist, or qualified healthcare professional before making substantial dietary, medical, or workout modifications.

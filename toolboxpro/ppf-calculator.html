Frequently Asked Questions

What is Public Provident Fund (PPF)?

PPF is a government-backed long-term savings scheme in India offering sovereign security, guaranteed annual compound interest, and complete tax exemption under the EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) category.

What is the current PPF interest rate in 2026?

The Ministry of Finance sets the PPF interest rate quarterly. Currently, the rate is 7.1% per annum, compounded annually on the lowest balance between the 5th and end of each month.

What is the maximum investment allowed in PPF annually?

The minimum deposit is ₹500 and the maximum limit is ₹1,50,000 per financial year, qualifying for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

Can PPF be extended beyond 15 years?

Yes, on completion of the initial 15-year tenure, you can extend your PPF account indefinitely in blocks of 5 years, with or without continuing fresh yearly contributions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I request a new tool or calculator?

Select "Request a New Calculator or Tool" in the form above and provide details on the mathematical formulas, typical inputs, and use cases you'd like to see. Our engineering team prioritizes high-demand utilities.

How do I report a mathematical error or formula bug?

Please specify the tool name, the exact input numbers you entered, the expected result according to official standards (e.g. RBI/GST Council), and the result displayed by ToolBoxPro. We audit and patch mathematical algorithms within 48 hours.

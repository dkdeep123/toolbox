Frequently Asked Questions

Why do computers use binary and hexadecimal?
Computers rely on physical transistors that hold two electrical states: on (1) or off (0). Hexadecimal (base 16) is used because each hex digit represents exactly 4 binary bits (nibble), making byte reading clean.
How do Roman numerals work?
Roman numerals use combinations of letters: I=1, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, D=500, and M=1000. Subtractive notation is used for numbers like 4 (IV) and 9 (IX).
What is the binary representation of decimal 255?
Decimal 255 is equal to binary 11111111 and hexadecimal FF.

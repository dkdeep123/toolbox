Understanding Your Loan EMI

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are applied to both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

1
Principal Loan Amount

The total sum of money borrowed from the bank or financial institution.

2
Annual Interest Rate (%)

The yearly percentage rate charged by the bank on your outstanding loan balance.

3
Loan Tenure (Years)

The total duration over which you plan to repay the borrowed amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the mathematical formula used to calculate EMI?
The standard mathematical formula is: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is Principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100), and N is tenure in total number of months.
Can I use this for home loans, car loans, and personal loans?
Yes. This EMI calculator works for any reducing-balance loan, including home loans, education loans, vehicle financing, gold loans, and personal loans.
How does loan tenure affect total interest payable?
A longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, but significantly increases the total interest you end up paying to the bank over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases monthly EMI but minimizes total interest.
Are prepayments or processing fees included in this calculation?
This calculator computes pure amortization EMI. Additional bank fees, documentation charges, insurance, and prepayments vary by bank and should be factored in separately.

Understanding Goods & Services Tax (GST)

Goods and Services Tax is a comprehensive destination-based indirect tax levied on the manufacture, sale, and consumption of goods and services. When computing invoices or analyzing receipts, you frequently need to either add GST (Exclusive) or remove GST (Inclusive).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?
The formula to extract GST from an inclusive amount is: GST Amount = Total Price - [Total Price × (100 / (100 + GST%))]. The original net amount is Total Price - GST Amount.
How is GST divided between CGST and SGST?
For intra-state transactions, the total GST is split equally: 50% goes to CGST (Central Government) and 50% to SGST (State Government).
Is this calculator suitable for invoicing and business accounting?
Yes, the formulas match standard Indian GST and international VAT accounting practices.

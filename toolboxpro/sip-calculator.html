How Does a Mutual Fund SIP Grow Your Wealth?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows everyday investors to invest fixed sums periodically into mutual funds without trying to predict stock market highs and lows. Over time, Rupee Cost Averaging and Compounding Returns combine to create exponential financial growth.

1
Monthly SIP Amount (₹)

Choose an amount you can comfortably invest every month (e.g. ₹2,500, ₹5,000, or ₹10,000). Consistency is key.

2
Expected Return Rate (% p.a.)

Historic long-term Indian equity mutual funds (large-cap, flexi-cap, index funds) have delivered 12% to 15% annualized returns over 10+ year horizons.

3
Investment Horizon (Years)

Longer horizons unleash compounding power. For example, investing ₹5,000/month for 15 years at 12% generates over ₹25 Lakhs from an investment of just ₹9 Lakhs!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the mathematical formula used for SIP calculations?
The standard mathematical compound formula is: M = P × [((1 + i)^n - 1) / i] × (1 + i), where:
  • M = Expected maturity value
  • P = Monthly investment amount
  • i = Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • n = Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12)
Can I increase my monthly SIP amount over time (Step-Up SIP)?
Yes! Many mutual funds offer a "Top-Up" or "Step-Up" SIP feature where your monthly investment automatically increases by a fixed percentage (e.g. 10%) each year as your salary increases, accelerating your wealth accumulation.
Are SIP returns guaranteed?
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks and returns are not guaranteed by government or banks. However, diversified equity funds held over 7 to 10+ years have historically provided resilient, inflation-beating returns.
Can I stop or pause my SIP anytime?
Yes. Mutual fund SIPs offer full flexibility. You can pause, modify the monthly amount, or stop your SIP anytime without any penalty fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for percentage increase or decrease?
The percentage change formula is: ((New Value - Old Value) / |Old Value|) × 100%. A positive outcome signifies a percentage increase, while a negative outcome indicates a percentage decrease.
How do I calculate what percentage of one number is another?
Divide the part by the total whole and multiply by 100: (Part / Whole) × 100%.
Can I use this for test score or academic grade percentages?
Yes, simply input your obtained score as the part and total possible marks as the whole.

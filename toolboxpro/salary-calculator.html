Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to an annual salary?
For a standard 40-hour workweek: Multiply your hourly wage by 2,080 (40 hours/week × 52 weeks/year). For example, $25/hour is approximately $52,000/year.
What is the difference between Gross Salary and Net Take-Home Pay?
Gross salary is the total agreed compensation before any subtractions. Net take-home pay is what actually arrives in your bank account after income taxes, PF, insurance, and professional taxes are deducted.
Can freelancers use this to estimate required hourly rates?
Yes. Divide your target annual earnings plus business overhead by billable hours to calculate your minimum hourly baseline.

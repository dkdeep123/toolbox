Frequently Asked Questions

How does this password generator create secure passwords?

It uses the browser's built-in cryptographic pseudo-random number generator (window.crypto.getRandomValues), ensuring high randomness that prevents brute-force predictability.

Are generated passwords saved or sent over the internet?

No. All passwords are generated purely within your local browser memory and are never transmitted, stored, or logged anywhere.

What makes a strong password in 2026?

A strong password has at least 16 characters, combines uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols, and yields over 70 bits of Shannon entropy.

What is password entropy?

Password entropy is a mathematical measure of how unpredictable a password is, calculated in bits. Passwords with over 80 bits are considered virtually unbreakable by modern supercomputers.

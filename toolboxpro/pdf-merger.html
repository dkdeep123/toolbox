Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data and PDF content safe while merging?
Yes, 100%. ToolBoxPro processes your PDF files directly in your web browser using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.
Can I rearrange the order of PDF files before merging?
Yes. Once you add your PDF documents, use the Up and Down arrow buttons to arrange them in the exact order you want them merged.
Is there a limit on file size or number of files?
Because processing is completed locally on your device, you can combine as many files as your device memory allows, completely free with no daily limits or sign-up required.
Does this work on mobile phones and tablets?
Yes, ToolBoxPro is fully responsive and functions smoothly on iOS (Safari), Android (Chrome), Windows, macOS, and Linux browsers.

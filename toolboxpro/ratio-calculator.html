Understanding Ratios and Mathematical Proportions

A ratio expresses the quantitative relationship between two numbers, indicating how many times the first value contains the second. Proportions state that two ratios are mathematically equal. They are ubiquitous in graphic design, photography aspect ratios (16:9, 4:3), cooking, and chemistry.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you simplify a ratio to lowest terms?
Find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of both numbers and divide each number by that GCD. For example, 12:18 divides by 6 to yield 2:3.
How do you solve a proportion like A:B = C:D for an unknown?
Use cross-multiplication: A × D = B × C. If D is unknown, D = (B × C) / A.
What are common aspect ratios used in digital media?
Common aspect ratios include 16:9 (widescreen monitors & YouTube), 9:16 (vertical mobile video & TikTok/Reels), 1:1 (square social posts), and 4:3 (classic displays).

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the live word count work?

The word counter analyzes your text in real time right in your browser, splitting tokens by whitespace and punctuation. No text is ever uploaded to a server.

What is the Twitter / X character limit in 2026?

Standard X (Twitter) posts allow up to 280 characters. Premium subscribers can compose longer posts up to 25,000 characters.

How is estimated reading time calculated?

Average adult reading speed is approximately 200 words per minute (wpm), while average speaking speed is roughly 130 words per minute.

Is my text private?

Yes, 100% private. All counting and analysis are performed locally inside your client browser. Your essays, articles, and confidential drafts never leave your device.

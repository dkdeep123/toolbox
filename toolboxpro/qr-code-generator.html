Frequently Asked Questions

Do generated QR codes expire?
No. The QR codes generated on ToolBoxPro are static QR codes. The encoded text, URL, or WiFi credentials are baked directly into the pixel pattern. They will never expire and do not depend on external redirect servers.
Can I download my QR code in vector SVG format?
Yes! ToolBoxPro provides both high-resolution PNG raster files and infinitely scalable SVG vector graphics, perfect for print media, posters, banners, and business cards.
How does the Wi-Fi QR code work?
When users scan a Wi-Fi QR code with their smartphone camera, iOS and Android automatically recognize the network credentials and prompt to connect without manually typing the password.
What is Error Correction Level?
QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction. Level L allows ~7% damage recovery, M allows ~15%, Q allows ~25%, and H allows ~30%. Higher levels make the QR code denser but more resilient if printed on outdoor materials.

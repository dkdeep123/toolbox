Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to ToolBoxPro (accessible at https://toolboxpro.in). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

All calculators and processing tools on ToolBoxPro (such as EMI, GST, Percentage, BMI, Image Compressor, and Unit Converters) run client-side in your web browser. We do not store, log, or send your calculation inputs, personal numbers, or uploaded images to external servers.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage strictly to save your visual preferences (such as dark mode vs light mode). We do not use persistent identifiers to track you across different websites.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to keep our tools 100% free. Google and other ad networks may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve relevant ads based on prior visits to our website and other sites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically collects standard server access logs (such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and date/time stamps) to maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and optimize uptime.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact Page or email at support@toolboxpro.in.

Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosure

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 2026

🔒 Summary of Core Privacy Guarantee:

ToolBoxPro operates on a Zero-Knowledge Client-Side Architecture. When you compress an image, merge a PDF, calculate an EMI, or convert units, your files and numeric inputs are processed entirely inside your local browser memory using HTML5, WebAssembly, and JavaScript. Your documents, photos, financial figures, and identity certificates are NEVER uploaded, stored, or viewed on our servers.

Welcome to ToolBoxPro (https://toolboxpro.in). We are dedicated to providing fast, transparent, and completely free online utilities while rigorously upholding your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how advertising and analytical partners operate on our site, and your rights under global privacy laws including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Information We Do NOT Collect (Client-Side Isolation)

Unlike traditional web services that require backend uploads for file processing, ToolBoxPro executes all primary tool logic on your client device:

  • Document & Photo Files: When you use our Image Compressor, Photo Resizer, PDF Compressor, PDF Merger, or Image to PDF Converter, files are loaded via the HTML5 File API directly into browser RAM. No bytes are transmitted across the internet to our servers.
  • Financial & Personal Calculations: Loan principal amounts, monthly income, SIP contributions, age, birth dates, and BMI metrics entered into calculator forms remain strictly in your browser session. We do not maintain any user database or calculation logs.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device to enable core website functionality and measure performance. ToolBoxPro utilizes cookies and local storage under the following categories:

  • Essential Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage solely to preserve non-identifiable user interface preferences, such as your chosen color theme (Dark Mode vs Light Mode) and ambient lighting toggles.
  • Analytical Cookies: We may use privacy-centric performance metrics to monitor aggregated page views, browser compatibility, and site performance to optimize load speeds.
  • Advertising Cookies: Third-party advertising partners place cookies to serve and measure contextual and personalized ads (see Section 3).

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policy

To support the high-performance hosting and ongoing development of ToolBoxPro without charging subscription fees or erecting paywalls, we display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and its certified advertising network partners.

Important AdSense Disclosures Required by Google:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to ToolBoxPro or other websites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the World Wide Web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google My Ad Center.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.

4. Log Files & Hosting Architecture

ToolBoxPro is hosted on high-reliability cloud edge infrastructure (such as Vercel and Cloudflare). Like standard web hosts, our hosting edge automatically logs standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic data:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (used for geographic edge routing and DDoS mitigation).
  • Browser type, device architecture, operating system, and language.
  • Referring and exit pages, timestamps, and HTTP response codes.

These server logs are used strictly for security monitoring, bandwidth management, and defending against automated bot attacks. They are never linked to any personally identifiable identity.

5. Rights Under GDPR (European Economic Area & UK)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we delete your data. Because ToolBoxPro does not collect or store user accounts, uploaded files, or personal inputs, we maintain zero personal records to delete.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may modify your cookie and personalized advertising preferences at any time through your browser settings or Google Ad settings.

6. Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act, California consumers have the right to:

  • Know what personal data categories are collected and shared.
  • Request deletion of personal data.
  • Opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do NOT sell user personal data. ToolBoxPro does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Advertising partners collect non-personal device identifiers via cookies subject to your opt-out rights described in Section 3.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

ToolBoxPro is intended for students, professionals, and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt remedial steps.

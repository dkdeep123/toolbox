Complete Scientific Calculator Reference & Formulas

Scientific calculators are essential for students, scientists, engineers, and financial analysts tackling equations beyond basic arithmetic. Below is a summary of standard scientific operations supported by this tool:

1
Trigonometric Functions & Angle Modes

Evaluate $\sin(\theta)$, $\cos(\theta)$, and $\tan(\theta)$ for right triangles and harmonic motion. Switch between Degrees (DEG, full circle = 360°) and Radians (RAD, full circle = $2\pi$) depending on your textbook or engineering problem.

2
Logarithmic & Exponential Calculations

Common logarithm $\log_{10}(x)$ determines the power of 10 needed to equal $x$, used in decibels, Richter magnitude, and pH levels. Natural log $\ln(x)$ uses base $e \approx 2.71828$, critical for compounding growth, decay, and calculus.

3
Combinatorics & Factorials

The factorial operator $n! = n \times (n-1) \times \dots \times 1$ powers permutations, combinations, binomial expansions, and statistical probabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between Degree (DEG) and Radian (RAD) modes?
Click the DEG / RAD toggle button at the top-left of the calculator toolbar. The active mode badge is clearly displayed on both the button and the calculator screen.
What trigonometric functions are supported?
The calculator supports sine (sin), cosine (cos), tangent (tan), inverse arcsine (sin⁻¹ / asin), inverse arccosine (cos⁻¹ / acos), and inverse arctangent (tan⁻¹ / atan).
How does the factorial (n!) operator work?
The factorial operator n! multiplies all positive integers down to 1 (e.g., 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120). It works on non-negative integers up to 170.
Are my calculations sent to a server?
No. All calculations are executed 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Zero formula or calculation data is ever transmitted across the internet.

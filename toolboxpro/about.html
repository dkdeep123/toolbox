About ToolBoxPro

We believe everyday online utilities should be lightning-fast, private by default, beautifully designed, and 100% free — without mandatory signups or invasive tracking.

Our Mission

ToolBoxPro was created to solve a common frustration: why should calculating loan EMI, converting image files, resizing documents, or generating QR codes require installing heavy software, creating user accounts, or uploading sensitive documents to unknown third-party servers?

We built ToolBoxPro as a clean, single-stop toolkit for students, developers, freelancers, accountants, and everyday internet users worldwide.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy

Your photos, PDF documents, and financial data are processed directly inside your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to our servers.

⚡ Blazing Speed

Built with lean, vanilla web standards for instant execution, zero bloat, and full offline Progressive Web App (PWA) readiness.

✨ No Paywalls or Signups

Every single calculator, converter, and PDF utility is completely free to use with unlimited operations.

📱 Mobile-First Design

Engineered for flawless responsiveness across mobile smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktops.

What We Offer

Our growing suite of free tools covers several everyday categories:

  • Financial Calculators: Standard reducing-balance EMI calculators, GST calculators (inclusive & exclusive), salary breakdown estimators, and discount counters.
  • Math & Measurement: Percentage increase/decrease, ratios, comprehensive scientific unit conversions, and number system converters.
  • Document & PDF Tools: Client-side PDF merger, image to PDF multi-page creator, and browser-based file compression.
  • Image Processing: Lossless and lossy image compression, exact photo & signature resizing for official government applications, and format converters.
  • Everyday Utilities: Exact age calculators, BMI index health assessments, date differences, and customizable QR code generators.

Commitment to Quality & Accuracy

All calculations are rigorously benchmarked against established mathematical algorithms, official standard banking formulas (such as RBI / SBI amortization schedules), and international SI measurement standards. We continually audit our code to ensure reliable, mathematically sound outputs.

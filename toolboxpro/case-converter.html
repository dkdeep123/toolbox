Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence Case?

Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence and proper nouns. Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every principal word, commonly used for headlines and book titles.

What is camelCase and snake_case used for?

camelCase (e.g. myVariableName) is the standard naming convention in JavaScript and Java, while snake_case (e.g. my_variable_name) is standard in Python, SQL, and database fields.
